# Remote Work Policy

Northwind Labs is a hybrid company. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are office days for every team, so that meetings and workshops can happen in person.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for a maximum of 20 working days per calendar year. Requests must be approved by the line manager and by People Operations at least two weeks in advance, because tax and insurance rules depend on the destination country.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, a monitor and a headset on their first day. A home office allowance of 300 euros per year covers a chair, a desk or other ergonomic equipment. Receipts must be uploaded to the expenses tool.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are from 10:00 to 16:00 Central European Time. Outside core hours, employees organise their schedule freely, as long as the team agrees on availability.
