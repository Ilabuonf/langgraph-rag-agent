# Travel and Expenses Policy

Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Economy class is the default for flights; business class is allowed only for flights longer than six hours.

## Meals and accommodation

During business trips the daily meal allowance is 45 euros in Spain and 60 euros abroad. Hotels are reimbursed up to 150 euros per night, or up to 220 euros per night in London, Paris and New York.

## Submitting expenses

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase, with a photo of the receipt. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary. Alcohol, fines and personal entertainment are never reimbursed.

## Training budget

Each employee has a yearly training budget of 1,200 euros for courses, certifications and conferences. Certification exams, such as cloud or language certifications, are paid in full when the exam is passed.
