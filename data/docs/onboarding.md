# Onboarding Guide

New employees join on a Monday. The first week is dedicated to onboarding: company introduction, account setup, security training and meetings with the team.

## Buddy programme

Every new hire is assigned a buddy from another team. The buddy answers practical questions and has a weekly coffee chat with the new hire during the first three months.

## Probation period

The probation period lasts six months. At the end of the third month the manager holds a mid-probation review to discuss goals, feedback and support needed.

## Holidays

Employees have 25 days of paid holiday per year, plus the public holidays of their office location. Holiday requests are made in the HR portal at least one week in advance.
